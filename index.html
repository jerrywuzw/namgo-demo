No cost, no inventory, no lock-in

Questions owners actually ask

What does this cost me?

Nothing up front. We cover design, manufacturing, storefront hosting, and shipping. You earn a royalty on what sells — if nothing sells, it costs you nothing.

What if I don't like the designs?

You get two free revision rounds on every design. Nothing reaches your storefront without your approval, and every design passes a human QA and IP check before it reaches you.

How long until my fans get their order?

Most items — stickers, pins, mugs, basic apparel — ship from a US partner in 3–6 days. A smaller set of specialty items runs through our overseas line at 18–25 days. That's shown as a date on the product page before checkout, never as a surprise after.

Am I locked into anything?

No contract lock-in. Pause or end the license whenever you want; we handle any orders already in flight.
